# Unweaver siege and reactive Loom QA

Comparison baseline: `16c0ad9`. Private Windows x64 Debug build; earlier QA packages and trusted Release builds are preserved. No merge or public release.

## Changes

- **Reactive Event Loom:** replaces the fixed duplicated lattice with four successive pairs of curved spindle pulses. Only the next pair reads current movement and prediction; every displayed trajectory remains locked throughout its own warning. Later folds can catch a change of route rather than leaving a static lane that is easy to avoid. Pulses continue from their curved endpoint along its tangent to the arena boundary, retaining swept collision and Needlecatch.
- **Fracture barriers:** a separate tactical option and part of Loom. Three split partitions mix cyan solid walls with pink, visibly spiked damage walls. Solids block player/boss movement, dash tunneling and hostile shots; spikes are traversable contact hazards that respect ordinary dash, immunity, damage reduction and Cocoon. Normal walls warn for one second, last six seconds and contain passage gaps. Warnings do not track the player. Friendly silk is preserved. Breaking a path by movement remains possible; the barriers do not add invulnerability to the boss.
- **Mobile supports:** the previous implementation explicitly restored support positions and zeroed velocity throughout committed boss attacks. That global freeze is removed. Uncommitted supports follow normal AI, steer around the boss footprint and detour around solid wall endpoints within their normal movement budget and arena bounds. Their own windups, bursts and artillery aiming keep their existing rules. Normal casts permit one support offensive commitment; escalation permits two. This is not a promise that every enemy moves during its own attack windup.
- **12% smaller boss:** body artwork, silk/damage radius and physical contact radius scale together. Damageable radius is 66.88 px, contact radius 28.16 px. Warnings, projectiles and world barriers remain at world scale. No generic HP, contact damage or movement-speed increase.
- **Hidden escalation at 5:00:** Phase IV after 36,000 fixed simulation ticks enables shorter readable warnings, short recovery, more aggressive contact objectives, up to three follow-ups and bounded support pressure (six active supports; twelve formation events). Existing committed warnings are unchanged. Real resolved player HP damage supplies bounded, decaying action utility; a kill adds terminal utility. Statistics reset with a new encounter/campaign. This is deterministic in-run tactical feedback, not persistent reinforcement learning; terminal reward cannot improve a fight that has already ended.
- **Eventual lethal territory pressure:** escalating spiked edge strips are separately warned before each fixed expansion, every ten seconds. By roughly forty seconds after activation they cover the arena. This deliberately permits eventual unavoidable coverage, as explicitly requested. It is a sudden-death pressure mechanism, not evidence that tactical planning alone guarantees a kill. Damage still uses normal combat rules; the boss remains damageable and zero HP still wins immediately. No player-facing kill-mode announcement is added.

Timed 1:00/2:00/3:00 phases, early victory, earlier bosses, campaign direction, upgrades and legitimate reward logic retain their existing behavior. QA scenarios suppress profile writes and legitimate unlocks.

## Verification and limits

- **46/46 MSVC Debug CTest suites pass.** New barrier and siege suites cover swept solids, warning/activation/expiry, traversable spikes, opening gaps, stationary artillery, transactional wall commitment, exact activation boundary, outcome utility/reset and scaled hitboxes. Existing tests cover timed phases, early victory, phase cleanup, campaign progression, signatures, Needlecatch and rewards.
- Loom tests cover later folds reacting while previously committed paths stay fixed, replay of admitted movement/dash responses, actual corner contact, interception, wall cleanup and earned-opening priority. Curved projectile tests cover exact next-step tangent velocity and preserve legacy curve expiry.
- Native mixed-wall and reactive-Loom captures were inspected. These establish rendering and distinct wall presentation, not human readability/fun acceptance.
- Four normal Phase IV formation samples recorded **1,111 / 1,328 / 1,508 / 421 support movement steps during boss warning/active states**, across distinct seeds/formations. This directly exercises the formerly frozen states.
- A native automated defense run starting at 5:00 lost after **1,865 active ticks (15.5 seconds)**, with three charge attempts, three formation events, 1,556 moving support steps, 163 HP of resolved damage reward and one terminal reward. This is one reproducible automated policy, not a human survival guarantee.
- The pressure-only test isolates ordinary damage/immunity/reduction and defensive dash timing, without boss attacks. It uses a stationary 16-rank defense build with an intentionally generous **50 extra HP test bonus**, not a 50 HP Cocoon barrier. Separate assertions exercise actual Cocoon capacity and dash protection. With defensive dash protection on cooldown, it died at tick 6,156 (51.3 seconds after activation). Neither test proves a universal one-minute survival bound with every signature/healing strategy.
- A fifteen-second real-time native Debug Loom/wall sample after two-second warmup: simulation CPU p99 **0.389 ms per rendered frame**, maximum **3.140 ms**; draw submission p99 **1.672 ms**; peak sampled private memory **55.7 MiB**. Startup discarded 229.7 ms; no discarded simulation time after warmup. These are short CPU samples on the current machine, not GPU timings, older-hardware certification or leak proofs.

Normal response admission checks use bounded movement/dash policies and current projectiles, walls and support velocity snapshots. They cannot guarantee every later autonomous support decision or every human response. Escalation explicitly relaxes safe-response admission as authorized; normal combat does not. No claim of a completed manual campaign or human-balanced revised fight is made.

Evidence: `portable-release/siege-qa`, `portable-release/siege-build.log`, `portable-release/siege-tests.txt`, and packaged launcher logs. All seventeen packaged launcher smoke checks passed across every selectable wave, all four phases, builds/seeds, forced Loom/barriers and 5:00 entry with performance logging. The real profile hash remained unchanged. These are startup/stepping checks, not a complete campaign playthrough.

## Testing controls

Use `Start-Unweaver.cmd` for normal varied combat, `Test-Event-Loom.cmd` for redesigned Loom, `Test-Barriers.cmd` for standalone walls and `Test-Kill-Mode.cmd` to start directly at 5:00. `Start-Wave-29.cmd` continues through Wave 35; `Select-Scenario.cmd` selects boss/wave/build/seed. F5 restarts the current fixture; P pauses. Logs include `OVERDRIVE` activation, damage/terminal reward and support movement diagnostics.

PowerShell examples: `./QA.ps1 -Wave 35 -FightSeconds 300 -Build defense -Seed 9`, `./QA.ps1 -Wave 35 -Phase 3 -Mechanic 6 -Build mixed`, or `./QA.ps1 -Wave 32 -Build mobility -Seed 42`. Add `-Performance` for a frame CSV. Forced mechanic fixtures restrict repertoire intentionally; assess the actual fight through the normal launcher.
